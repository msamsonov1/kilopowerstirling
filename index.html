Analytical Formulation: Free-Piston Stirling Linear Alternator Core Dynamics

NASA Kilopower surface fission systems rely on Free-Piston Stirling Engines (FPSE) directly coupled to linear alternators. The power piston reciprocates hermetically without mechanical crankshaft linkages. To achieve 10+ year maintenance-free lunar/Mars service, radial clearance gaps ($g_0 \approx 500\text{--}600\,\mu\text{m}$) must be safeguarded against Maxwellian unbalance forces that cause catastrophic side-pull and cylinder friction welding.

1. Unbalanced Magnetic Pull (UMP) & Instability

When the cylindrical magnet array undergoes a radial displacement $\delta y(t)$, the air gap reluctance shrinks on one side and expands on the opposite. The resulting Maxwell magnetic stress produces an attractive lateral side force $F_{\text{UMP}}$ that opposes mechanical centering:

$$F_{\text{UMP}}(y) = \frac{B_g^2 A_{\text{pole}}}{2\mu_0} \cdot \left[ \left(\frac{g_0}{g_0 - \delta y}\right)^2 - \left(\frac{g_0}{g_0 + \delta y}\right)^2 \right] \approx \frac{2 B_g^2 A_{\text{pole}}}{\mu_0 g_0} \cdot \delta y$$

This introduces a negative magnetic spring stiffness $k_{\text{mag}} = -\partial F_{\text{UMP}}/\partial y$. If $|k_{\text{mag}}| \ge k_{\text{flexure}}$, the mechanical flexure bearing goes unstable, accelerating the reciprocating piston directly into the beryllium-copper stator bore within 3 engine cycles (50 ms).

Key Threshold: $k_{\text{crit}} = k_{\text{bearing}} - \frac{2 B_g^2 A}{\mu_0 g_0} > 0$

2. Instantaneous $(v, B)$ Orbital State Observer

The axial stroke velocity $v(t) = \dot{z}(t)$ induces back-EMF $\mathcal{E}(t) = -N \frac{d\Phi}{dt} = -N \frac{\partial \Phi}{\partial z} v(t)$. Concurrently, non-invasive radial pickup coils monitor the normal flux density $B_r(\theta, t)$. In nominal concentric alignment, the phase space trajectory forms an orthogonal Lissajous ellipse:

$$\Phi_{\text{net}}(t) = \Phi_0 \cos(\omega t), \quad v(t) = -\omega z_{\text{max}} \sin(\omega t + \phi)$$ $$\left( \frac{B_r(\theta, t)}{B_{\text{peak}}} \right)^2 + \left( \frac{v(t)}{v_{\text{peak}}} \right)^2 = 1 + \eta(\delta y, \theta)$$

An eccentricity perturbation $\delta y$ produces an asymmetric odd-harmonic distortion $\eta(\delta y, \theta)$. The DSP extracts the area asymmetry between the upper and lower orbital halves to infer sub-micron clearance shifts in real time.

Observer Latency: $\tau_{\text{dsp}} = 2.4\,\text{ms}$ (Discrete Kalman Filter at 50 kHz sampling)

3. Active Quad-Sector Centering Trim Algorithm

To neutralize $F_{\text{UMP}}$ without physical wear, the stator laminations are wound with auxiliary 4-quadrant centering coils. The DSP injects an offset bias current $I_{\text{trim}}(\theta)$ that modulates local saturation:

$$I_{\text{trim}}(\theta) = - K_p \cdot \Delta B(\theta) - K_d \frac{d(\Delta B)}{dt} - K_i \int \Delta B \, dt$$ $$F_{\text{restore}} = \sum_{k=1}^4 \frac{\mu_0 N_{\text{aux}}^2 I_{\text{trim}, k}^2 A_{\text{aux}}}{4 (g_0 \pm \delta y_k)^2} \cos(\theta_k)$$

The closed-loop compensator drives the differential flux $\Delta B = B_{\text{top}} - B_{\text{bot}} \to 0$. This stabilizes the equilibrium point even if planar flexure diaphragm springs experience creep or micro-cracking during 88,000 continuous hours of surface operation.

Current Draw: $\le 14.5\text{ W}$ auxiliary power (0.35% of 4.2 kW Stirling output)

4. Nuclear Radiation & Thermal Tolerant Substrates

Surface nuclear operations (KRUSTY / Kilopower architecture) subject the alternator to fast neutron fluences ($> 10^{14}\,\text{n/cm}^2$) and gamma doses ($> 2\,\text{Mrad}$). Magnetic properties degrade predictably according to thermal remanence coefficients:

$$B_r(T) = B_{r0} \left[ 1 + \alpha_{\text{SmCo}} (T - T_0) \right] \cdot \exp\left(-\frac{\Phi_n}{\Phi_{\text{crit}}}\right)$$ $$\mu_r(\text{core}) = \mu_{r0} \cdot \left(1 - \beta_{\text{rad}} \gamma_{\text{dose}}\right)$$

The magnet array employs ultra-high coercivity $\text{Sm}_2\text{Co}_{17}$ magnets with reversible thermal coefficient $\alpha = -0.035\%/\text{K}$ operating up to 573 K. Pick-up sensor tracks feature inorganic alumina/silicon nitride insulation to avoid radiation-induced polymer breakdown.
